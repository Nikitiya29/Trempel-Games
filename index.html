Our Mission

We are Trempel Game, an independent game studio founded in 2026 by Nikita Donorkin (Nikitiya). We believe that the best games are forged not in corporate boardrooms, but by passionate players. Our lab creates high-end indie experiences built uniquely for those who truly understand gaming.

🎮

Player First

Every mechanic and design choice we make is driven by what players actually enjoy, free from predatory monetization.

✨

High-End Indie

Delivering premium aesthetics, immersive atmospheres, and polished gameplay mechanics usually reserved for AAA titles.

🚀

Innovation

We push the boundaries of mobile and wearable game development, bringing fresh ideas to established genres.
